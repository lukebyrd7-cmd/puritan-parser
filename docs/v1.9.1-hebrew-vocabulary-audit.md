# v1.9.1 — Hebrew Vocabulary Audit

## Scope and source boundary

This audit compares all 1,903 frequency-ordered entries in Miles V. Van Pelt and Gary D. Pratico, *The Vocabulary Guide to Biblical Hebrew and Aramaic*, Second Edition (Zondervan, 2019), with Puritan Parser’s Hebrew vocabulary system.

VGBH was used privately as verification-only: it identified entries requiring attention, but its definitions were not imported, committed, or made canonical. Distributed corrections are supported independently by the project’s existing contributor-authored Hebrew gloss source and its recorded public-domain consultation of Strong’s Hebrew Dictionary (1890). The uploaded transcription is not present in the repository. The sanitized machine-readable result is `audits/v1.9.1-hebrew-vocabulary-audit.json`; it contains no VGBH definitions.

## Method

The local audit parser extracts the VGBH number, pointed and normalized headword, frequency, explicit POS, verbal stems, alternate-spelling metadata, cross-references, and morphological-note structure in memory. Only non-expressive audit facts are written to the sanitized output.

Mapping prefers PP’s stable `lemma:hebrew:<lexical-id>` identity. Pointed spelling, consonantal normalization, OSHB/Strong identity, POS, frequency, and homograph competition contribute separate evidence. Spelling alone cannot authorize a homonym mapping. PP seed rows are secondary to canonical numeric OSHB lexical identities.

Gloss comparison uses controlled lexical-domain equivalences and primary/additional-sense position to prioritize review. Lack of lexical overlap becomes `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW`, not an automatic error. Every distributed semantic correction was then checked manually against the approved Strong’s consultation source. No correction is based on string similarity or VGBH alone.

Frequency comparison is diagnostic only. PP frequency was not changed. The audit uses frequency-aware absolute and ratio thresholds and treats large differences as possible segmentation or identity problems.

## Learner-facing consistency

Corrections are resolved in the shared gloss model rather than written into `vocab_all.json`. Learn, flashcards, Reader Word Details, Word Page, Global Search, Browse/Search, Custom Deck practice, and Needs attention all use the shared effective gloss. Search text now includes that effective corrected presentation.

Personal gloss behavior is unchanged. “Use my glosses” still replaces the standard presentation, while “Add my glosses” appends personal senses. No SRS, mastery, status, Needs attention, personal-gloss storage, or migration schema changed.

## Deferred review

The audit intentionally leaves 169 missing PP entries, 64 ambiguous mappings, 64 PP homonym collisions, 3 possible duplicates, 149 lower-confidence leading mappings, 422 primary-order candidates, and 591 semantic comparisons for future source review. These categories overlap at the entry level. They remain machine-readable and frequency-prioritized; they were not silently dropped.

The remaining work requires better lexical-identity/source data, especially for bound prefixes, number forms, homographs represented by one Strong identity, and low-frequency forms absent from the current PP vocabulary rows. No biblical corpus file, `vocab_all.json`, user-owned data, account feature, or synchronization feature was changed.
