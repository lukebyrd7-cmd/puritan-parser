## Resolution rules

Source strings are split conservatively on explicit semicolon-style separators and on the existing source comma convention, trimmed, and deduplicated case-insensitively while preserving first spelling and source order. Standard, additive personal, and replacement personal modes produce one effective presentation. Occurrence glosses never enter this resolver.

The resolver performs no writes. Personal edits change only `pp_personal_glosses`; they do not touch scheduling, mastery, Needs attention, Reader locations, or canonical source records.
