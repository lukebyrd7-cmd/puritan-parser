# v1.9.3 — Bilingual Lexical Completion & Integrity

## Result

The lexical completion gate passes: Greek is 5,478/5,478 and Hebrew is 9,152/9,152, with zero unavailable standard lexical identities and 100% lexical Reader-token coverage in both languages. Contextual-gloss data was not expanded or promoted into lexical answers.

The machine-readable authority is `audits/v1.9.3-bilingual-lexical-completion.json`. It contains 14,630 sanitized stable-identity records, provenance keys, mapping confidence, review status, frequency tiers, proper-name policy, Reader coverage, and deterministic QA samples. It contains no private dictionary definition text.

## Baseline and remediation

The reviewed completion manifest exhausts all categories. Abbott-Smith supports 670 final records; the pinned Open Scriptures Strong’s digital edition supports the remaining 99. The previously reviewed `αἴρω` order remains `remove; take up; lift`.

Hebrew began with 9,151/9,152 structurally covered. The sole unavailable row, `lemma:hebrew:i` / `hb-28058`, is the interrogative הֲ particle; it now has the functional learner gloss `question marker; whether?`, supported by OSHB morphology and Gesenius §100 without changing the stable source identity. All 9,152 identities were included in the full semantic/integrity classification. The v1.9.1 VGBH-reviewed population and its 14 correction decisions remain regression-protected. Known `ruddy i` and `’ i` extraction artifacts were removed from standard source data.

## Identity, homonym, and proper-name policy

Case, canonical lemma, POS, Strong association, corpus occurrence, Abbott-Smith, approved Strong’s data, and private verification were used together. Common/proper pairs such as `γάζα`/`Γάζα`, `σμύρνα`/`Σμύρνα`, and `φοῖνιξ`/`Φοῖνιξ` remain separate. The five prior disagreement records were manually resolved in the reviewed manifest.

Proper names are lexically covered with conventional English forms while remaining independently excluded from ordinary vocabulary practice. The audit reports 559 Greek and 3,170 Hebrew proper-name identities, all covered and all ordinary-practice excluded. Reader, Search, and Word Page lookup remain available.

## Audit and regression protection

The permanent tests derive totals from canonical vocabulary inventories and require complete coverage, usable English, zero source-script fallback, complete provenance, resolved identities, proper-name separation, practice coherence, privacy boundaries, and deterministic samples. Samples include 100 frequency-2–9 identities, 100 hapax identities, and 50 proper names/special cases for each language; all final sample classifications are `GOOD`.

Greek Reader coverage is 137,554/137,554 lexical tokens. Hebrew Reader coverage is 306,785/306,785 lexical tokens. Punctuation and structural tokens without a lexical lemma are outside the denominator.

Greek remains at zero contextual gloss values. Existing Hebrew occurrence-level contextual behavior is unchanged. No biblical corpus file or `vocab_all.json` file was regenerated or modified.
